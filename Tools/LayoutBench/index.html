The ring is 8 pt wider than its frame. SwiftUI centres a stroke on its path, so the Ø94 CPR ring actually reaches 70 → 172 pt. The earlier Bench drew it as a CSS border inside the box, which is why your spacing looked right here and crowded on the watch. It is drawn correctly now — the ring you see is the ring you get.

Fans export as Swift. Switch to Fans, place a submenu, then Generate: you get the literal Fan(slots:…) block to paste over that fan's .arc(n) line in FanLayout.swift. Fans you have not touched stay .arc(n) and keep rendering exactly as they do today — a unit test enforces it. Positions read out in fan space (194 × 191) because that is what the file stores, while the canvas stays in screen points so the pucks and the clock line up.
